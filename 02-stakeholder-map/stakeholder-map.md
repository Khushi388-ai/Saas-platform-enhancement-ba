# Stakeholder Map

*Fictional roles for a simulated case.*

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loan applicant (customer) | Fast, transparent approval | Medium | Keep satisfied via UAT feedback and surveys |
| Branch / loan officer | Less manual work | Medium | Involve in requirement workshops |
| Credit underwriter | Accurate risk decisions | High | Collaborate on eligibility rules |
| Compliance / risk officer | KYC, audit trail, regulation | High | Review and approve requirements |
| Product owner (retail lending) | Business outcomes, ROI | High | Sponsor, sign-off |
| IT / development team | Clear, feasible requirements | Medium | Regular refinement sessions |
| QA / UAT team | Testable requirements | Medium | Review acceptance criteria |
| Customer support | Fewer status calls | Low | Inform, gather pain points |
